# FastForward TM - PDF Organizer Setup Guide for Team

## For Your Team Members (Simple Instructions)

### After the app is live at `fileinput.fastforwardtm.com`:

1. **Go to the website**: https://fileinput.fastforwardtm.com
2. **Use the app; the API key is securely configured on the server** (Courtney will provide one shared key for the team)
3. **Upload your PDF packet** (the one from the client)
4. **Click "Process PDF"** - wait 30-60 seconds
5. **Download the organized PDFs** - they're sorted in the order Courtney wants
6. **Done!** PDFs are ready to add to your transaction file

---

## What Happens:

✅ All pages from the messy PDF packet get split into individual documents  
✅ Documents are automatically sorted in this order:
   1. Pre-Approval Letter or Proof of Funds
   2. Agency Disclosure
   3. Team Disclosures
   4. Purchase Agreement
   5. Any Addendums
   6. Property Disclosure
   7. Lead Paint Disclosure
   8. Affiliated Business Arrangements
   9. Other documents

✅ Key data gets extracted (dates, names, addresses, loan info, etc.)

---

## Troubleshooting

**"Website won't load"**
- Make sure you're going to: `fileinput.fastforwardtm.com` (not the main site)
- Try refreshing the page

**"API key invalid"**
- Copy the key exactly - no extra spaces
- Ask Courtney if you're unsure

**"Processing takes too long"**
- Large PDFs (100+ pages) can take a minute or two
- Just wait, it's working
